About Us

Building technology-driven solutions for early health risk awareness.

Our Mission

SwasthSathi exists to bridge the gap between rural communities and early health awareness. In India, millions of people discover chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension only after symptoms become severe—often too late for preventive care.

We believe that early screening should be accessible to everyone, regardless of location, literacy, or economic status. Our platform is designed to support timely medical consultation, not replace it. We use technology responsibly to empower individuals with awareness, helping them take the first step toward better health.

Supporting doctors, not replacing them

The Problem We Are Solving

Late Diagnosis

Most chronic diseases are detected only after complications arise, when treatment becomes costly and complex.

Lack of Awareness

Early symptoms of diabetes and hypertension often go unnoticed, especially in rural areas with limited health education.

Accessibility Barriers

Rural India faces challenges like distance to clinics, lack of transport, and shortage of healthcare workers.

Overcrowded Systems

Public hospitals are overwhelmed with patients, leaving little time for preventive care and early screening.

Our Ethical Commitment

Not a Diagnostic Tool
SwasthSathi is a screening platform, not a medical diagnosis system.

Supporting Awareness
We empower users with early risk awareness to seek timely medical consultation.

Professional Guidance Required
Final medical decisions must always be taken by certified healthcare professionals.

We are committed to using technology responsibly, transparently, and ethically in healthcare.
