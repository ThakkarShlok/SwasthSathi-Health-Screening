Read before you book

SwasthSathi does not recommend, rank or endorse any doctor.

  • Results are returned live from public map data and shown in the order that service provides. We do not reorder them.
  • Ratings and review counts are written by the public on that platform. They are not a measure of clinical quality.
  • A listing here is not a check on anyone's qualifications. Verify registration, specialty, availability and fees yourself before consulting.
  • Listings can be out of date, closed or wrongly categorised. Call ahead.
  • Your government primary health centre or district hospital is also a valid first stop, and is usually free.
